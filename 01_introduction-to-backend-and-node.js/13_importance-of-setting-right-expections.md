### Recommended
- Set your expectation according to the time you have, to **dedicate to the batch**.
- Assume: (if total 150 hours), then - 1 hr/day ➜ 5 months.

- `research, experimentation, exploration, practice ➜ 4 hours/day.`

- **Student ➜** 2 hours/day
